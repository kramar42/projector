A note that is not work. It carries no `status` and no `priority`, so it never appears on a
lifecycle board and never gets counted as something outstanding — most of a vault looks like
this.

`status` and `priority` are declared `expected: true`, so the triage axis knows this note is
unfiled — and the answer to that is often "nothing to do". A thought is allowed to just sit
there.
