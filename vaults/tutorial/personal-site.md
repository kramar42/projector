A project card. It is a project because it carries a `project:` block — nothing declares a
"kind" anywhere, and no facet says `type: project`.

Other cards join it with the `project` facet, and the **Projects** table rolls up what they
say. Cards can nest under it with `parent:` too: membership and decomposition are separate
questions, so a card can belong here and sit under another card.
